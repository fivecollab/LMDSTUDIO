Hello,

L.M.D Studios establishes the creative practice and vision of John Leon Morales, a designer with a multicultural background based in Geneva, Switzerland.

Founded in 2022, its role is to serve as a space for continuous experimentation across his personal projects. With fashion as the main medium, his work explores clothing codes in a contemporary context, combining ideas from streetwear, art, music, and popular culture. Each piece reinterprets familiar archetypes through colors, fits, and materials — using design as a language to articulate an intention or a message shaped into garments and essential everyday objects.

Beyond John’s personal work, his practice extends into a wider field of graphic design, art direction, product development and visual storytelling. Grounded in a community-driven approach, he collaborates with artists and creative partners on commissions for cultural and commercial clients, offering thoughtful and sharp consulting services.

Services

Fashion Design, Product Development, Art Direction, Visual Identity, Websites, Ecommerce, Graphic Design, Brand & Campaign Visuals, and Creative Consultancy.
